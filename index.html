Featured Project 02 • Mobile UI/UX Case Study

DigiSub — Smart Subscription & Billing Telemetry

An in-depth UI/UX case study addressing subscription fatigue, unwanted auto-renewals, and fragmented payment tracking. Explored from user research to final high-fidelity design system.

The Subscription Fatigue Dilemma

Modern digital consumers maintain between 10 to 18 active subscriptions across entertainment, cloud storage, software SaaS, gym memberships, and food apps. Over 78% of users report losing money on services they forgot to cancel before billing renewal.

Core User Problem:

"I never know when an app is going to charge me until I get an unexpected bank SMS debiting my account."

UX Solution Pillars:

01
Proactive 3-Day Alerts: Notification reminders with 1-tap direct cancellation deep links.
02
Monthly Spending Breakdown: Clear visual telemetry categorized by Entertainment, Work, and Utilities.
03
Shared Family Plan Splitting: Automated calculation of who owes what in group plans.

Alex Mercer, 27

Software Engineer & Tech Enthusiast
Goals: Keep track of 14+ dev & entertainment subscriptions, prevent unwanted auto-renewals.
Frustrations: Hidden cancellation buttons buried inside 6 layers of app settings menus.
"I want one simple screen where I can see every dollar going out this month."

Priya Sharma, 24

Graduate Student & Freelance Writer
Goals: Split Netflix, Spotify, and Adobe Cloud subscriptions with 3 roommates fairly.
Frustrations: Awkward monthly reminders to ask friends for their share of the bill.
"Splitting subscriptions manually in WhatsApp chats is chaotic and awkward."

Card & Plan Telemetry

Live card balance, active plans counter, and instant toggle between personal and shared plans.

Smart Warning System

Color-coded tags (Red for renewal within 48h, Yellow for this week, Green for safe).

One-Tap Cancellation

Direct deep-link integration taking users directly to the vendor's cancellation confirmation screen.

Design Tokens & Component Architecture

Built with atomic component hierarchy ensuring scalable light and dark mode implementations:

Primary Active #10B981 Affirmation, Active status
Indigo Accent #6366F1 Action buttons, Brands
Urgent Warning #F43F5E Immediate renewals, Deletions
Midnight Slate #0F172A Text, Elevated cards

Evaluated with 12 test users across 5 standard tasks.

-42% Faster Cancellation

Time-to-cancel dropped from 3m 40s to just 42 seconds.

100% Task Completion Rate
