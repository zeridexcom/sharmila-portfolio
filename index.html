Hello! I am Sharmila P, a creative and detail-oriented UI/UX Designer based in Bangalore, India. My mission is to bridge user needs and business goals through thoughtful, human-centered design.

With specialized expertise across User Research, Wireframing, High-Fidelity Prototyping, and Usability Testing, I thrive on untangling complex workflows into seamless, intuitive interfaces. Whether it's architecting a mobile SaaS application, designing a conversion-optimized e-commerce platform, or crafting brand identity systems, I obsess over every micro-interaction and pixel.

I actively augment my design workflow with state-of-the-art AI tools including Antigravity, Bold AI, Bookipi, and Design Mantic — allowing me to iterate rapidly from conceptual research to high-fidelity clickable deliverables.
